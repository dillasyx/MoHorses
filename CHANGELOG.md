## 1.0.0 – Initial release

The horse breeding of Mo' Creatures, rebuilt for Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric, NeoForge and Forge.

- 15 horses: Zebra, Zorse, Zonkey, Nightmare, Wild Nightmare, Bat Horse, Unicorn, Pegasus, Dark Pegasus, Fairy Horse (16 colours), Undead and Skeleton Unicorns and Pegasi, Ghost Horse and Ghost Pegasus
- Breeding and transformations with the Essences of Light, Darkness, Fire and Undead, also on vanilla horses
- Undead horses rot into skeletons, burn in daylight and keep monsters off their riders
- Ghosts of tamed rare horses, flying on winged horses, the Nightmare's fire trail, the Unicorn's charge
- Five amulets to carry horses (and other tamed pets) around
- Crystal Horse Armor, whip, sugar lumps, hearts, unicorn horn and the Shuffling music disc
- English, German, Spanish, French and Italian
