## 1.0.7

- Wild Unicorns: very rarely and always alone, in Flower Forests, Meadows, Lush Caves, Cherry Groves, Old Growth Birch Forests and Groves, never near another unicorn. They are peaceful until attacked, then they fight back; they can't be tamed, and drop their horn and leather
- Zombie Horses, Undead Unicorns and Undead Pegasi keep out of the sun: by day they look for shade nearby and stay in it (not with armor on, and not while ridden or on a lead)
- Unicorns, Fairy Horses and Nightmares glow in the dark: the horn and the gold, the wings in their colour, and the embers (brighter during a charge, or after redstone). The red in a Dark Pegasus's coat and feathers smoulders. The eyes of the Unicorns, Fairy Horses, Dark Pegasi (red) and ghosts sparkle in their colour. Ghosts shimmer faintly at night
- In the dark a soft aura surrounds the Dark Pegasi and ghosts, and faintly the Unicorns. They and the Fairy Horses (in their colour) give off a few particles, Nightmares small flames
- Winged horses lower their legs again when they come close to the ground, ready to land. Dust puffs up under their hooves when they take off and touch down, Pegasi and Dark Pegasi lose a feather now and then, and tiny bats flutter off from Bat Horses

## 1.0.6

- Every horse of the mod has a voice of its own, made from the Mo' Creatures horse, donkey and zebra sounds and cut and levelled like the vanilla ones. Only the Undead and Skeleton Unicorns and Pegasi still sound like vanilla zombie and skeleton horses
- Maggots squish when hurt or killed, instead of sounding like a hurt player, and crawl without a sound
- Winged horses sweep their legs back and paddle with them in flight (not when they just jump)
- The feathered wings no longer clap together over the back while a winged horse climbs
- Crystal Horse Armor as in Mo' Creatures: only the Bat Horse, Unicorn, Pegasus, Dark Pegasus, Fairy Horse and the ghosts wear it. Vanilla horses and zombie horses can't put it on any more, and Undead and Skeleton Unicorns and Pegasi wear no armor at all (they drop the one they have on)
- A Zombie Horse that rots into a Skeleton Horse drops its armor instead of keeping it out of reach (skeleton horses have no armor slot). Armor that is already stuck on one comes off with shears
- The Shuffling music disc plays again: its sound file was damaged in every release so far

## 1.0.5

- With [Modonomicon](https://modrinth.com/mod/modonomicon) installed (optional), an in-game guide book explains taming, breeding, essences, the undead and the items in five chapters, with the horses shown in 3D. Craft it from a book and a sugar lump
- New advancement tab "Mo' Horses" with 23 advancements along the breeding line, the undead, ghosts and the items, up to the Stable Master challenge
- Wild Nightmares spawn much less often, alone, and only in the Nether Wastes, Soul Sand Valleys and Basalt Deltas (no longer in the forests, where they made up most of the monsters)
- Now and then a Wild Nightmare comes with a Piglin Brute riding it, golden spear in hand
- Zebras drop leather, like horses
- Wild Nightmares drop leather, and with a 15% chance a Heart of Fire when a player kills them (Looting raises the chance)
- Chests as in Mo' Creatures: only the Zonkey, the Pegasi (Pegasus, Dark Pegasus and their undead, skeleton and ghost forms) and the Fairy Horse carry one. Other horses drop theirs, with everything in it

## 1.0.4

- Breeding follows Mo' Creatures more closely: Unicorn + Dark Pegasus now gives a black Fairy Horse (the Dark Pegasus comes back in a Sky Amulet)
- Removed two crosses that Mo' Creatures doesn't have: Unicorn + Pegasus fed a pumpkin no longer gives a Nightmare, and Nightmare + Pegasus no longer gives a Dark Pegasus
- Dark Pegasi fall in love with Essence of Darkness
- Every Fairy Horse can now be dyed once, whatever colour it was born with (black ones and coloured foals too); white dye turns it white
- New whip recipe: leads, leather and iron nuggets, with a single unicorn horn as the handle (no iron ingot, one horn instead of three)
- The amulet preview no longer cuts off the ears of vanilla horses and other animals that hold their heads high

## 1.0.0 – Initial release

The horse breeding of Mo' Creatures, rebuilt for Minecraft 26.3 on Fabric, NeoForge and Forge.

- 15 horses: Zebra, Zorse, Zonkey, Nightmare, Wild Nightmare, Bat Horse, Unicorn, Pegasus, Dark Pegasus, Fairy Horse (16 colours), Undead and Skeleton Unicorns and Pegasi, Ghost Horse and Ghost Pegasus
- Breeding and transformations with the Essences of Light, Darkness, Fire and Undead, also on vanilla horses
- Undead horses rot into skeletons, burn in daylight and keep monsters off their riders
- Ghosts of tamed rare horses, flying on winged horses, the Nightmare's fire trail, the Unicorn's charge
- Five amulets to carry horses (and other tamed pets) around
- Crystal Horse Armor, whip, sugar lumps, hearts, unicorn horn and the Shuffling music disc
- English, German, Spanish, French and Italian
